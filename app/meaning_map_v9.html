what this is

Every word here is a point in a space of meaning. Words that mean similar things sit close together; the closeness is measured, not eyeballed.

You stand on one word, name a few fields you'd like to reach, and the map hunts for the words sitting in between — the stepping stones from where you are to where you pointed.

try this first: type a word, add 2–3 fields you want to connect it to, and watch it find the words in between.
